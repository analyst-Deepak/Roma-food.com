COOKIE POLICY

Effective Date: 1st January 2026 | Last Revised: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how ROMAS Chocolates (Roma Foods) utilizes cookies, local storage, and similar technologies on https://romaschocolates.com/.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device by websites you visit. They help sites remember your preferences and ensure smooth navigation across pages.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary: Essential for site navigation, sample bag state persistence, and security verification.
  • Performance & Canvas Buffering: Used to buffer sequence frames for our cinematic 1080p chocolate player without excessive bandwidth consumption.
  • Analytics: Anonymous aggregated metrics on popular product profiles to assist in cocoa inventory planning.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can adjust your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to reject or delete cookies at any time. Please note that disabling cookies may reset items stored in your tasting sample tray.
